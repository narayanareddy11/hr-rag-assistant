Annual Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year.

Leave accrues at a rate of 2 days per month.

Employees should request annual leave through the leave management system at least 5 working days before the planned start date.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for up to 6 months after the end of the calendar year, subject to manager approval.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after successfully completing the probation period.

Remote work must be coordinated with the employee's manager and team.

Sick Leave Policy

Employees may take sick leave when they are unable to work because of illness.

More than three consecutive days of sick leave requires a medical certificate.

Probation Policy

New employees are subject to a probation period.

During probation, either the employee or the company can terminate employment with 15 days notice.

Performance Review Policy

Employee performance reviews are conducted annually.

Performance ratings include Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, and Needs Improvement.

Promotion Policy

Employees may be considered for promotion based on performance, responsibilities, business requirements, and demonstrated skills.

Employee Benefits

Eligible employees may receive company benefits according to their employment terms and applicable company policies.

Employees should contact HR for benefits-specific information.
